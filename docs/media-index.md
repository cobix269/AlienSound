## Preparation

- Photos are resized to 1600 pixels wide for repository display and re-encoded as JPEG with metadata removed.
- Videos are remuxed into MP4, retaining the source H.264 video and first AAC audio stream without re-encoding. Auxiliary data streams and source metadata are omitted.
- Audio-only M4A files contain that same AAC stream without re-encoding or sound processing.
- Preview JPEGs are extracted at 2 seconds.
- The GIF is a silent excerpt of IMG_2967 from 2 to 7 seconds, at 8 fps and 320 pixels wide.
- No generated image is used as documentary evidence of the circuit.
